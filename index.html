Meet the owner

Hamare ghar ki rasoi, ab aapki bhi

In 2022 I started frying small batches at home. The nimki recipe is the one my mother-in-law made for every Diwali, and I still remember waiting beside the kadhai for the first hot piece.

Neighbours kept asking for more, so a corner of Purana Bazar became Mahavir Snacks. Four years later I still taste every batch before it is packed.

The aim is simple. Swad jo rahe yaad, so you remember it long after the packet is empty.

  • Fried fresh and packed on the day you order
  • Aata (whole wheat) options for Suhali and Nimki
  • Sarson tel bhujiya and ghee theekwa, made the old way
  • Clear per kg rates, no surprise at the counter
  • Clean kitchen, sealed packs, honest weight
